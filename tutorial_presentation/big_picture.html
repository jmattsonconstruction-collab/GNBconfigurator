One Estimate Flows Automatically Into Your Job Schedule

Configurator

A standalone HTML file you open on any device. It works offline at job sites, allowing you to create estimates anywhere.

Google Sheet

Acts as your database. When you finalize an estimate, the job data is sent here automatically.

Ops Board

Reads from the Google Sheet and displays all your jobs as draggable cards on a digital war board.

Transfer Code

A simple code (like "muffin33") lets you pull jobs between devices without any login required.

Email Backup

An automatic email backup is sent to greatnorthernborders@gmail.com every time a job is finalized.
