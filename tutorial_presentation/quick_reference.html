Estimate → Transfer Code → War Board → Job Sheet → Email Backup

1.

Create Estimate

Open configurator, fill out estimate, and click "Send to Ops Board".

2.

Get Code

Receive your simple transfer code (e.g., muffin33).

3.

Sync to Board

Open War Board on any device, enter code, and the job appears.

4.

Update Status

Drag the job card to "In Progress" when you start work.

5.

Add Details

Click the card to open the Job Sheet and fill in extended details (colors, prep).

6.

Finalize

Drag to "Complete" or click "Finalize" to automatically send the email backup.

⏱ Total time from estimate to scheduled job: under 60 seconds
