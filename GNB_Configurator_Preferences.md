# GNB Configurator — Design & Feature Preference Questions

Boss, after analyzing the different versions of the GNB Configurator (from the original `configurator.html` to the latest `index.html` and the `rebuilt_local_configurator.html`), I've identified several visual and functional variations. 

To ensure the final "pro" version aligns perfectly with your vision, please review the following questions and let me know your preferences:

## 1. Visual Style & Theme
*   **The "Clean Pro" Look (Rebuilt Version):** Uses a modern, high-contrast interface with a blue/slate color palette, radial gradients, and "floating" cards with deep shadows. (Seen in `rebuilt_local_configurator.html`)
*   **The "Natural Border" Look (Latest Index):** Uses the signature GNB green, with a more traditional layout and simpler card designs. (Seen in `index.html`)
*   **Question:** Do you prefer the **modern blue "software app" look** or the **traditional green "GNB brand" look**? Or should we combine the modern "pro" layout with the GNB green branding?

## 2. Header & Branding
*   **Minimalist Header:** A simple green bar with the company name and a live price banner. (Latest `index.html`)
*   **Hero Section:** A large top section with an "eyebrow" tag, a logo wrap, and a descriptive subtitle (e.g., "Concrete border estimate in minutes"). (Rebuilt `rebuilt_local_configurator.html`)
*   **Question:** Should the top of the app be a **slim, functional header** to save space on mobile, or a **prominent hero section** that reinforces the brand and project goal?

## 3. Navigation & Flow
*   **Step-by-Step (Wizard):** Users must complete one step (e.g., Customer Info) before moving to the next (e.g., Stamp). (Latest `index.html`)
*   **Single-Page (Scroll):** All sections (Customer, Profile, Stamp, Extras) are visible on one long scrolling page, with a live summary sidebar. (Rebuilt `rebuilt_local_configurator.html`)
*   **Question:** Do you prefer the **focused step-by-step wizard** (better for small screens) or the **all-in-one scrolling dashboard** (faster for experienced users)?

## 4. Feature Highlights
*   **Image Handling:** Some versions use base64 encoded images directly in the background, while others are set up for admin-uploaded URLs.
*   **Question:** For the product images (Stamps/Profiles), do you want them to be **large and prominent** (like in the original `configurator.html`) or **compact and secondary** to the text (like in the latest `index.html`)?

## 5. Summary & Output
*   **Overlay Job Sheet:** A dedicated, full-screen "Job Sheet" view for printing and final review. (Latest `index.html`)
*   **Sidebar Live Summary:** A summary that updates instantly in a side column as you make selections. (Rebuilt `rebuilt_local_configurator.html`)
*   **Question:** Do you want the final estimate to be presented as a **separate "Job Sheet" overlay** or as a **live-updating sidebar** that's always visible?
